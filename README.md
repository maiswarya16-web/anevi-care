# 🩺 Anevi Care

**Care • Guidance • Community**

**AI-assisted healthcare for frontline workers**

Anevi Care is an AI-assisted multilingual community health assistant designed to support frontline health workers with health information, screening guidance, warning-sign identification, referral support, patient records, and follow-up assistance.

## Problem

Frontline health workers such as ASHA and ANM workers often work in communities where they need quick access to reliable health information and practical guidance.

Important information may be spread across training materials, guidelines, and different systems. This can make it difficult to quickly find appropriate information during field work.

## Our Solution

Anevi Care is a community health assistant that helps frontline health workers:

* Find relevant health information quickly
* Understand health information in simple language
* Identify potential warning signs and emergency situations
* Support appropriate referral decisions
* Maintain patient information and consultation records
* Track important patient vitals
* Provide first-aid guidance
* Interact using multiple Indian languages
* Use voice input for easier interaction

## Target Users

* ASHA workers
* ANM workers
* Community health workers
* Other frontline healthcare workers

## Key Features

1. Multilingual AI health assistant
2. Health information and guidance

9. Voice-based interaction
10. Simple conversational interface

## Safety

Anevi Care is designed as an information and decision-support tool.

It does not replace qualified medical professionals, clinical diagnosis, prescriptions, or emergency medical services.

Emergency situations should always be referred to appropriate emergency medical services and qualified healthcare professionals.
