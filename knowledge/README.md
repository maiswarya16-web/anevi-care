# Anevi Care Trusted Knowledge

This folder contains trusted healthcare knowledge used by Anevi Care to provide grounded and reliable guidance.

## Purpose

The knowledge base will contain trusted healthcare resources such as:

- Government health guidelines
- Maternal and child health guidance
- Immunization information
- First-aid guidance
- Warning signs and referral guidance
- ASHA and ANM training resources
- Other verified healthcare resources

## Important

Only trusted and verified healthcare information should be added to this folder.

Anevi Care does not replace qualified healthcare professionals or emergency medical services.
